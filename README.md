# ZEC FUND

### Creator fees go straight into Zcash

ZEC FUND is built around one simple idea:

**turn creator fees into ZEC.**

Instead of treating creator fees as the end of the story, ZEC FUND uses them to build a Zcash treasury.

---

## How it works

The concept is simple:

**TRADING**
↓
**CREATOR FEES**
↓
**TREASURY**
↓
**ZEC**

1. Creator fees are generated through trading
2. Fees are directed to the project treasury
3. Treasury funds are used to acquire ZEC
4. Treasury activity is made publicly verifiable

The goal is to continuously build the ZEC treasury as creator fees are generated.

---

## Why Zcash?

Zcash was created around a different idea of digital money:

**financial privacy.**

Zcash supports shielded transactions, allowing users to transact without publicly exposing all transaction details when using shielded addresses.

ZEC FUND is built around this narrative by directing creator fees toward ZEC.

Learn more about Zcash:

https://z.cash/

---

## The problem

Most creator-fee tokens stop at generating fees.

The fees become revenue for the creator, but they don't necessarily create a transparent, visible treasury or a long-term asset accumulation strategy.

ZEC FUND takes a different approach:

**creator fees → treasury → ZEC**

One mechanism.

One treasury.

One goal.

## What ZEC FUND is NOT

ZEC FUND is not:

* a promise of profit
* a guaranteed investment
* a financial product
* an official Zcash project
* affiliated with the Zcash Foundation or Electric Coin Company

ZEC FUND is an independent community project built around the idea of directing creator fees toward ZEC.
