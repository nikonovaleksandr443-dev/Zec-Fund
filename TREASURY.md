## How Treasury Funds Work

Creator fees are intended to be directed toward the treasury.

Treasury funds are then used to acquire ZEC.

The intended flow is:

**CREATOR FEES**
↓
**TREASURY**
↓
**ZEC PURCHASE**
↓
**ZEC TREASURY**

---

## Transparency

Treasury data will be updated as purchases occur.

Each purchase should include:

* date
* amount used
* amount of ZEC acquired
* approximate ZEC price
* transaction reference

This page will not include purchases that have not actually occurred.
